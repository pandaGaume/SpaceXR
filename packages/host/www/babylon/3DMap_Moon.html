About This 3D Moon Map

This is a 3D map of the Moon using Babylon.js and the SpaceXR library. Navigate using mouse or touch inputs.

The map displays hillshaded albedo imagery of the lunar surface at up to zoom level 7. Points of interest include Apollo landing sites, major craters, maria (lunar seas), mountain ranges, rilles, and future exploration candidates.

Tile data is sourced from OpenPlanetary, USGS, and NASA lunar reconnaissance datasets.

Moon Map Navigation Help

Mouse (on the map)

  • Left-click + drag: Move the map
  • Right-click + drag: Rotate the map
  • Mouse wheel: Zoom in/out

Outside the map area, the default camera behavior of Babylon.js still applies.

Touch (on the map)

  • 1 finger drag: Move the map
  • 2 fingers pinch: Zoom
  • 2 fingers rotate: Rotate the map

Touch gestures outside the map area will interact with the Babylon.js scene as usual.
